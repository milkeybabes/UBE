UBE v1.6c Camera Lens Visual
===========================

Adds an educational text diagram to the Camera inspector.

For perspective cameras with physical camera fields it shows:
- focal length and sensor size
- approximate horizontal/vertical/diagonal FOV
- 35mm equivalent focal length
- a simple frustum/cone diagram
- plain-English lens feel: wide, normal, telephoto/narrow

For orthographic cameras it shows the fixed rectangular view concept.

This is an educational approximation. Unity gate fit/projection matrix can alter the exact final view.

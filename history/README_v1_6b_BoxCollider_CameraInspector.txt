UBE v1.6b - BoxCollider + Camera Inspector
=========================================

Adds first-pass educational inspectors for:

- BoxCollider
- Camera

BoxCollider inspector shows local center/size/min/max, trigger state, physics material, layer overrides, and explains that the box is invisible physics/interaction volume rather than the rendered mesh.

Camera inspector shows enabled state, clear flags, background colour, depth/render order, lens/projection fields, clipping planes, viewport, culling mask, target texture/display, and common HDR/MSAA/occlusion flags.

This is deliberately inspection-only. No camera preview or collider overlay yet.
